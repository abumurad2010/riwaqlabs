Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 May 2026

The short version: Iwan Dock collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers. Everything Iwan Dock needs lives on your Mac and never leaves it.

What we collect

Nothing. Iwan Dock does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We do not use analytics or tracking tools, we do not embed third-party SDKs that gather information, and Iwan Dock does not require an account or sign-in of any kind.

What stays on your Mac

Your panels, layouts, themes, workspaces, and preferences are stored locally on your device through standard macOS app storage. This information is yours. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is removed if you delete the app.

The Automation permission

Iwan Dock can perform system actions such as putting your Mac to sleep, locking the screen, or restarting. To do this, macOS may ask for permission to send Apple Events (the system's "Automation" permission). This is a standard macOS prompt and you are always in control of it.

  • The permission is used only to carry out the system action you triggered.
  • Iwan Dock does not read, store, or transmit anything through this permission.
  • If you decline, Iwan Dock keeps working normally — only those specific system-action buttons are unavailable. Everything else degrades gracefully.
  • You can review or change this anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

Network use

Iwan Dock does not send your information over the network. The app is distributed through the Mac App Store, which handles purchase and update delivery under Apple's own privacy terms.

Children

Iwan Dock is a productivity utility suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Because Iwan Dock collects no data, we expect changes to be rare.

Contact
