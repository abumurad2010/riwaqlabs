Support

Quick answers, and a real person at the other end of the email.

How do I open my panels? +

Click the Iwan Dock icon in your menu bar to show or hide your panels. Panels float over your desktop and stay where you put them, so they reappear in the same place next time.

How do I rearrange, group, or add apps? +

Drag any icon to reorder it within a panel — the panel opens a gap to show where it will land. To group apps together, hold the Option (⌥) key while dragging, and Iwan Dock fuses them into a group. You can also drop a file directly onto an app to open it there.

To add or remove panels, right-click a panel and choose Add Panel or Remove Panel.

What are workspaces? +

A workspace remembers which panels are showing and where they sit. Build one for Focus, one for Meetings, one for Off-hours, then switch between the whole set with a single keystroke.

How do themes work? +

Iwan Dock ships with six looks — Iwan, Graphite, Parchment, Midnight, Sand, and Slate. Themes are applied per panel, so each panel can have its own mood. You can also choose From Image and hand Iwan Dock a photo or wallpaper; it reads the dominant tones and dresses the panel to match.

Why is Iwan Dock asking for an Automation permission? +

System actions like sleep, lock, restart, and log out use macOS Apple Events, which require the Automation permission. macOS will prompt you the first time you use one. Iwan Dock uses this only to carry out the action you triggered — nothing is read or sent.

If you'd rather not grant it, that's fine: Iwan Dock keeps working and only those specific system-action buttons are unavailable. You can change your choice anytime in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

Can I hide a panel and bring it back later? +

Yes. Right-click a panel and choose Hide. Hidden panels wait in the menu bar — open Show Hidden Panel and pick the one you want back. You can also lock panel positions so they don't move by accident.

Does Iwan Dock collect any of my data? +

No. Iwan Dock collects nothing — no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Your layouts and preferences stay on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

What does Iwan Dock cost, and is there a subscription? +

Iwan Dock is a one-time $4.99 purchase on the Mac App Store. No subscription, and updates are included.

What do I need to run it? +

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on either Apple silicon or Intel Macs. Iwan Dock is a native AppKit app, sandboxed and signed, and ships as a universal binary.
