What it does

Everything within reach. Nothing in your way.

Iwan Dock keeps your apps in floating panels you arrange once and forget. It stays out of sight in the menu bar, then appears exactly where you left it.

Floating panels

Group apps into clean, draggable panels — one per project, client, or part of your day. Spin up as many as you like.

Across or down

Lay a panel out horizontally along an edge or vertically down the side. Switch in a click; every panel keeps its place.

Drag, group, drop

Drag an icon and the panel opens a gap to show where it lands. Hold Option to fuse two apps into a group — or drop a file onto an app to open it there.

Six built-in looks

Iwan, Graphite, Parchment, Midnight, Sand, and Slate — hand-tuned palettes that look right from the very first launch.

Theme from any image

Hand it a photo, a wallpaper, a colour you love — Iwan Dock reads the dominant tones and dresses the panel to match.

Every panel, its own mood

Themes are per-panel, never app-wide. Calm dark for writing, warm for the daily stuff, bold for the side project — all on screen at once.

Hide & show, instantly

Right-click a panel to tuck it away. It waits in the menu bar and reappears the moment you ask for it.

Workspaces

Each workspace remembers which panels are showing and where they sit. Jump from Focus to Meetings to Off-hours with one keystroke.

System actions

Sleep, lock, restart, and log out sit right beside your apps. The things you reach for most, one click away.
