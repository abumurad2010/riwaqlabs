Corda Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Who We Are

Corda is a family connection application. Contact us at privacy@riwaqlabs.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Account & Profile Data

Name, display name, date of birth, gender, profile photo, family role, and contact information provided during setup.

Family Space Data

Family name, invite codes, family member relationships, and membership roles.

Content You Share

Posts, photos, videos, voice notes, locations shared in the feed, reactions, and comments.

Calendar & Events

Event titles, dates, times, participants, locations, and reminders you create.

Wellbeing Data

Daily mood check-ins. Individual mood scores are private by default and never shared with other family members.

Financial Data

Expense titles, amounts, categories, and family member assignments you log manually. We do not collect bank account numbers, card numbers, or any payment credentials.

Children's Data

For members identified as minors (under 18), we collect the same categories above. Parental controls allow adult family members to manage children's access. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 4.

Location Data

If you enable the Circle feature, we collect your device GPS coordinates and share them with members of your family space only. Location data is not shared with third parties.

Usage Data

App session duration, features used, and screen time for parental controls. Stored locally on your device.

Device Data

Device locale and region used to determine language, currency, and available services. We do not collect device identifiers for advertising purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Provide and improve the Corda app experience
  • Enable family communication and coordination
  • Generate AI-powered suggestions via our AI provider
  • Send notifications you have opted into
  • Enforce parental controls and screen time limits

We do not use your data for advertising.
We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. AI Features

Corda uses an AI service to power smart features including dinner suggestions, recipe assistance, and trivia generation. When you use these features, relevant context such as dietary preferences and general prompts is sent to our AI provider for processing. We do not send personally identifiable information such as names, photos, or contact details to AI services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Within your family space (data visible to members you invite)
  • With service providers necessary to operate the app
  • When required by law

6. Children's Privacy

Corda includes a Kids Mode designed for children aged 4 and above:

  • Individual children's wellbeing data is never visible to other family members
  • Parental controls allow adults to manage all children's access and content
  • We do not serve advertising to any users including children
  • We do not share children's data with third parties for commercial purposes
  • Parents may request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting privacy@riwaqlabs.com

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. Family space data is synchronized between family members' devices. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data in transit and at rest.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all your data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@riwaqlabs.com to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes through the app. Continued use of Corda after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Email: privacy@riwaqlabs.com
